# tsdb 0.9.0 benchmark: interim results (2026-11-02)

**These are interim results. More is coming.** The release benchmark is still running. What is below is measured and
checked; what is missing is listed at the end. The finished results will replace this page and go into the README.

The full tables, with every measurement, its spread and how each system was set up, are in
[0.9.0-interim-tables.md](0.9.0-interim-tables.md). This page picks out the main numbers.

## How it was run

- One machine: AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (16 cores, 32 threads), 64 GB RAM, NVMe disk. One system at a time.
- Every server gets the same memory limit: 48 GB. A server that runs out of it, or refuses a query because of it,
  is shown as such. That is a result, not a missing number.
- Every answer is checked against tsdb's before it is timed. A system whose answers differ is not timed.
- Every timing is repeated 5 times. Where the page says "median", it is the median time of one request.
- The data: 100 series of sensor readings, 1 million readings each (100 million rows), from seven kinds of sensors
  with realistic timing jitter. Phase 5 uses 10,000 series of 10 million readings (100 billion rows).
- "Batch of 100" means one request asks about 100 series. tsdb answers that as one request; SQL databases get one
  query for the 100 series.

## 100 billion rows: tsdb and ClickHouse

10,000 series x 10 million readings, loaded by 8 streams. ClickHouse's queries ran after its background merges had
settled.

| | tsdb | ClickHouse |
|---|---:|---:|
| size on disk | **137.8 GB** (1.38 bytes per row) | 212.1 GB (2.12 bytes per row) |
| insert | **912 s** (110 M rows/s) | 1,937 s (52 M rows/s) |
| insert until merges settled | **3,379 s** | 4,507 s |

Median time of one query, tsdb over TCP:

| query | rows | tsdb | ClickHouse |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| one random row | 1 | **0.32 ms** | 2 ms |
| aggregate a window of one series | 1 M | **0.60 ms** | 8 ms |
| aggregate one whole series | 10 M | **0.11 ms** | 35 ms |
| aggregate 100 series | 1 G | **1.3 ms** | 723 ms |
| aggregate the whole table | 100 G | **91 ms** | 70.5 s |
| one series by hour | 10 M | **17 ms** | 42 ms |
| one series by minute | 10 M | **28 ms** | 54 ms |
| 100 series by day (Europe/Oslo) | 1 G | **156 ms** | 1,071 ms |
| 100 series by hour | 1 G | **368 ms** | 1,100 ms |
| whole table by day (UTC) | 100 G | **0.27 s** | 108.9 s |
| whole table by day (Europe/Oslo) | 100 G | **40.7 s** | 108.3 s |
| whole table by hour | 100 G | **53.0 s** | 112.9 s |

tsdb answers most aggregates from statistics it keeps per block of 8192 readings, so where the question lines up
with those blocks (whole series, whole table, UTC days) it reads very little. Hours, minutes and local days that do
not line up have to read the data; there tsdb is 2 to 3 times faster.

ClickHouse's times here are whole milliseconds (what its benchmark tool reports), so the small queries are
approximate.
